## Committed direction: Signal Blue Mobility

- **Design movement:** Corporate mobility control-room; operational clarity over decoration.
- **Core principles:** Trust, hierarchy, calm under pressure, explainability, reversible actions.
- **Color philosophy:** Ink navy backgrounds; cyan for navigation and active data; amber for warnings and attention; lime for verified/safe; red only for emergency and destructive state.
- **Layout paradigm:** Fixed left rail on desktop, bottom navigation on mobile, 12-column content grid, high-value hero status row, modular cards with clear section headers.
- **Signature elements:** Route-line motif, numbered rescue stepper, status chips, mono transaction IDs, toll checkpoint timeline, compact confidence/disclosure banners.
- **Interaction philosophy:** One obvious next action per workflow, confirmation for emergency/payment simulation, optimistic UI only for reversible preferences; payment and verification wait for authoritative demo-service state.
- **Animation:** Restrained 150–220ms transitions, progress line motion only during rescue, no distracting looping animation.
- **Typography system:** Inter/system sans for UI, tabular numerals for financial metrics, small monospace for IDs and audit references.
- **Brand essence:** A calm co-pilot between the driver, toll operator and highway system.
- **Brand voice:** Direct, reassuring, transparent about what is simulated.
- **Wordmark/logo:** FASTSAFE wordmark paired with a flat road-shield symbol: two cyan road lanes creating a negative-space shield on an opaque navy square.
- **Signature brand color:** Signal cyan `#18D4C5`.

Custom imagery is not materially needed for this functional dashboard prototype beyond the required project-specific logo.
